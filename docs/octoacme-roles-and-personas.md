# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers / Tech Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers and Tech Leads translate product and project priorities into technical delivery plans, capacity decisions, and team-level execution. They help the engineering organization deliver reliably while balancing quality, speed, and sustainability.

### Responsibilities
- Align technical planning with roadmap and milestone priorities
- Manage team capacity, staffing, and workload distribution
- Resolve technical dependencies and identify delivery risks early
- Support architecture, design quality, and implementation standards
- Coach developers and guide decision-making in ambiguous situations

### Goals
- Deliver work predictably and with strong engineering quality
- Maintain healthy team execution and sustainable delivery pace
- Improve technical clarity across product and project stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering planning and cross-functional delivery reviews
- Dependency and risk discussions with Project Managers and Product Managers
- Technical design decisions and developer coaching conversations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to assess feasibility and sequencing trade-offs.
- Coordinates with Project Managers on timelines, risks, and cross-team dependencies.
- Supports Developers by clarifying architecture, priorities, and delivery standards.

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA and Test Leads define how product quality is validated throughout the project lifecycle. They ensure that release confidence matches acceptance criteria, user needs, and operational readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage approach, and validation criteria
- Review acceptance criteria and identify edge cases before release
- Triage defects, coordinate bug fixes, and assess risk severity
- Lead release readiness checks and quality sign-off decisions
- Partner with teams to improve test automation and repeatable validation

### Goals
- Reduce defects that reach customers and production users
- Increase confidence in product quality before release
- Support predictable, low-risk delivery milestones

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and defect triage meetings
- Release readiness reviews and quality checkpoints
- Collaboration with Developers and Product Managers on acceptance criteria

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers to validate fixes, identify regressions, and clarify quality expectations.
- Provides Project Managers with release confidence and risk signals that affect schedules.
- Works with Product Managers to confirm that user-facing outcomes are validated against business intent.

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads protect the organization from technical, operational, and regulatory risk. They help teams design secure systems and ensure that product work meets required standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review systems for security risks, vulnerabilities, and design gaps
- Define secure coding practices, review checkpoints, and compliance requirements
- Support risk assessment for sensitive features, data handling, and regulated workflows
- Coordinate remediation plans for high-risk issues and incidents
- Provide guidance on privacy, policy, and governance needs

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance exposure across the project lifecycle
- Build trust with stakeholders and end users
- Ensure secure delivery without unnecessary operational friction

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and governance checkpoints
- Risk and remediation updates for Product and Project leadership
- Compliance-related escalation and mitigation planning

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Engineering Managers and Developers to embed secure practices into delivery.
- Advises Product Managers on risk trade-offs and policy constraints.
- Informs Project Managers and stakeholders of timing, dependency, and escalation impacts tied to compliance work.

---

## Customer Success / Support Leads

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Leads connect product delivery to real user impact. They surface feedback, adoption issues, and support trends so the team can improve experience and operational readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback, support trends, and adoption blockers
- Translate user pain points into actionable product and process improvements
- Coordinate support escalations and triage for major issues or customer impact
- Help define success and readiness criteria for launch and post-release support
- Share operational learnings with product and delivery teams

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Reduce friction in adoption and day-to-day use
- Ensure customer impact is visible to product and delivery teams

### Typical Communication
- Feedback reviews, customer issue triage, and release readiness discussions
- Support summaries and adoption insights for Product and Project teams
- Stakeholder updates tied to customer impact and launch preparedness

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides Product Managers with customer signals and evidence of value or friction.
- Helps Project Managers assess operational readiness, release risk, and communication needs.
- Supports Developers and QA with real-world scenarios that should be validated before or after launch.

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads make outcomes measurable and help the team understand whether a project is creating business and user value. They support decision-making with evidence and clear success metrics.

### Responsibilities
- Define product and project metrics, dashboards, and reporting standards
- Support experimentation, analysis, and measurement planning
- Review adoption, quality, and performance trends after release
- Ensure instrumentation and data collection are aligned with business goals
- Help teams interpret results and drive iterative improvements

### Goals
- Make impact measurable and visible to stakeholders
- Improve evidence-based prioritization and decision-making
- Connect delivery work to business outcomes and customer value

### Typical Communication
- Metrics reviews, reporting updates, and decision support sessions
- Data quality and instrumentation planning with engineering teams
- Outcome reviews with Product Managers and project leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Product Managers by validating whether goals and success metrics are being met.
- Helps Project Managers communicate milestone progress and readiness using measurable signals.
- Partners with Developers and QA to ensure instrumentation, reporting, and validation are in place.

---

## Release Managers / Operations Owners

### Role Summary
Release Managers and Operations Owners coordinate the practical work of launching and supporting changes in production. They ensure that deployment windows, operational checks, rollback paths, and communications are prepared and managed effectively.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release windows, deployment sequencing, and rollback strategies
- Coordinate smoke tests, post-deploy validation, and incident readiness
- Monitor production health and support a fast response to launch issues
- Align launch communication with Project Managers and stakeholders
- Capture operational learnings for future release improvement

### Goals
- Reduce operational risk during release and post-launch follow-up
- Improve confidence and continuity for production service quality
- Keep release execution predictable and well-communicated

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning, launch reviews, and incident readiness check-ins
- Operational status updates with delivery and stakeholder teams
- Post-release verification and follow-up communication

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and QA to ensure release readiness and smoke-test coverage.
- Collaborates with Product Managers on launch timing, messaging, and success validation.
- Coordinates with Project Managers to align release milestones, stakeholder updates, and contingency plans.
